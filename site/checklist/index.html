Before you fly with this

This is a transcription of the N61574 checklist card, laid out for the iPad. It is a convenience aid, not an approved document.

The Pilot’s Operating Handbook and FAA-approved Airplane Flight Manual carried in the aircraft are authoritative. Where this app and the POH disagree, the POH wins. Verify the numbers on the Reference page against your POH before relying on them, and keep a paper checklist aboard as backup.

Checklists you add or edit here are your own work product and are not reviewed by anyone. Check your edits on the ground.
